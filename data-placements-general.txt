OBJECTIVE / VISION / MISSION
Objective: create career awareness, build employability skills and job-search strategies, identify potential employers, organize career-planning activities, bridge students-alumni-employers, and feed industry feedback into curriculum.
Vision: "Educate – Enhance – Empower Employment – Entrepreneurship skills by team work"; shift from knowledge-based to skill-based employment.
Mission: raise career awareness, build employability via training in tech/communication/analytical/professional skills, support entrepreneurship, strengthen student-alumni-industry ties, and continuously improve via employer feedback.

TRAINING MODULES
I. Communication Skill Enhancement
II. Effective Placement Training (+ Personality Development)
III. Model Aptitude Tests
IV. Group Discussions
V. Mock Interviews
Also: 10-day Campus Residential Program (June), Career Guidance Seminar for pre-final years, Value Added Courses, Online Assessments, and "Placement Training in Collaboration with FACE Prep" covering aptitude, logical reasoning, soft skills, and interview prep.
